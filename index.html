    Bin Night

    Set up your bin days

    Bin Night works for any address in Midlothian. It needs the next collection date for each of your bins, and it works out every date after that.

    Find them automatically

    Opens Midlothian Council's bin page. Enter your postcode and choose your address, and Bin Night picks up the dates.

    Or type them in

    Look up your address on the council's bin page and copy the next date for each bin. Leave a bin blank if you don't have it.
